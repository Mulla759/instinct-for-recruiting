# Rejection recovery

## What it does
Rejections get logged, triaged, and answered. The ones worth saving get a short follow-up that keeps the door open; the rest get filed and the tracker moves on.

## How it runs
The agent watches the inbox for rejections, logs each one in the tracker with date and stage, and flags the ones where a reply makes sense: a referral in play, a warm contact, a final-round cut. It drafts a short note - thank them, ask to stay in touch, restate interest in future roles - and stages it for approval. Nothing sends on its own.

## What you get back
No rejection slips by unread, every door that can stay open gets a nudge, and the tracker stays honest about what is dead.
