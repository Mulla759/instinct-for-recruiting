# Opportunity tracker sheet

## What it does
A Google Sheet that captures roles worth pursuing before they become applications: company, role, link, source, fit, deadline, and the next move. It is the intake half of the search; the application pipeline tracker is the other half.

## How it runs
Everything the watches, sweeps, and one-off finds surface lands here first. The agent logs rows as it finds them, dedupes against what is already tracked, and promotes a row to the application tracker the moment you say go. Stale rows get pruned so the sheet stays a queue, not a graveyard.

## What you get back
One sheet that answers "what should I apply to next" in ten seconds, sorted by fit and deadline.
