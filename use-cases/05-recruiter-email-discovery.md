# Recruiter email discovery

## What it does
Finds real email addresses for outreach targets. The rule: never guess. A bounce or a wrong-person send is worse than no send.

## How it runs
An address is used only when it is published, comes from a reply or invite you received, or is derived from a pattern you confirmed at that company (for example, a supplied first.last address grounding others at the same domain). Pattern-derived but unverified addresses get labeled as exactly that.

## What you get back
A contact sheet separating verified addresses from unverified derivations from LinkedIn-only contacts, so you always know which is which before anything sends.
