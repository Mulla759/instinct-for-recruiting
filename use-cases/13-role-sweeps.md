# Sweep passes for new roles

## What it does
Wide sweep passes for roles beyond what the tracker already holds: startup boards, VC job boards, and company career pages outside the daily watch's usual sources.

## How it runs
Sweeps run as broad passes and filter hard: genuinely new, currently open, right level for your graduation date. Anything already in the tracker is skipped, so sweeps only ever add.

## What you get back
A batch of net-new roles the daily sources missed, logged into the tracker with links, rather than a re-sorted list of roles you already saw.
