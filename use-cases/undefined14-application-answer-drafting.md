# Application answer and essay drafting

## What it does
Writes the long-form parts of applications: portal essays, "why this company" answers, and open-text questions, kept copy-paste ready in one doc.

## How it runs
Answers are built from real material: the resumes, actual project outcomes, real numbers. Where an application leaves a field like a referral name open, it stays open rather than being filled with a guess. Essays for portals that ban AI assistance are not written at all; prep material is built instead.

## What you get back
A doc of paste-ready answers per application, so a portal session is assembly, not writing from scratch under a timer.
