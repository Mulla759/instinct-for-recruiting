# LinkedIn SEO and AEO audit

## What it does
Repositions a LinkedIn profile so it surfaces in recruiter search and AI answers, aimed at the role family you are targeting without touching what is true.

## How it runs
Headline options are benchmarked against profiles that actually rank in recruiter search, not asserted from a formula. Copy rules: no "aspiring," no "candidate," nothing that reads as asking permission. Lead with the discipline and let credentials do the proving.

## What you get back
A headline and About-section rewrite with the benchmark behind each choice, and a list of which profile fields move ranking versus which are decoration.
