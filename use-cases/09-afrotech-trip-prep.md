# AfroTech trip prep

## What it does
Turned a conference (AfroTech, Houston, November 2-6) into a recruiting trip: flights researched, recruiters ranked, and outreach prepped before wheels up.

## How it runs
Flight options were priced and ranked, then handed back for the pick. Nothing was booked; the purchase decision stays with you. Sixteen ranked recruiters each got a pre-written LinkedIn DM and an email follow-up in a single doc, ready to send on the road.

## What you get back
A shortlist of flights to choose between and a 32-message pre-conference doc, so the trip is spent talking, not typing.
