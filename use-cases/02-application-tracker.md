# Application pipeline tracker

## What it does
One Google Sheet holds every role: company, track, stage, dates, links, notes. It is the single place that knows what is applied, what is drafted, what is waiting on you, and what died.

## How it runs
The tracker updates as work happens, not as a separate chore. New drops from the watch get logged, submitted applications move stages, clarified rows get corrected. A second sheet tracks VC and startup roles sourced from accelerator boards (a16z, YC Work at a Startup, Techstars) alongside company career pages.

## What you get back
A sheet you can open any time and know the whole state of the search in ten seconds. Fit scores sit next to roles when assessed, so the next move is obvious.
