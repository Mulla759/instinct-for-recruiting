# Cold recruiter outreach

## What it does
Writes and stages cold emails to recruiters, hiring managers, and founders. Drafts sit in your Gmail until you approve each one by name. Nothing sends on its own.

## How it runs
The framing depends on the target. Recruiters get pressed for the next stage: an assessment, a referral to the hiring team, something to do. Never a coffee chat. Alumni and community contacts (ColorStack, MLT) get a genuine referral ask instead of a pitch. Shared affiliation leads where a real one exists: school alumni first, then community.

Every claim in a draft is grounded in the actual resume. When there is no verified email address, the draft still gets written with the intended recipient and their LinkedIn URL at the top of the body, so the To line can be swapped later.

## What you get back
Staged Gmail drafts, trimmed and bulleted, eagerness up front. You send them one at a time: "send the one for X," and it goes.
