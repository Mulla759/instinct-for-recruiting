# Interview and assessment logistics

## What it does
Tracks the moving parts after an application lands: online assessments, recorded interviews, deadlines, and the calendar entries around them.

## How it runs
Assessments (HackerRank, HireVue, recorded screens) get logged with their due dates and status in the tracker. Events and recruiting fairs go straight onto the calendar with registration links and eligibility notes attached. Term mismatches get caught: an assessment invite saying Summer 2027 against a posting mirror saying January 2027 got flagged for a clarifying email instead of being left to break later.

## What you get back
No silent deadlines. Every assessment has a status, every event has a calendar entry, and every mismatch between what a company says in two places gets surfaced.
