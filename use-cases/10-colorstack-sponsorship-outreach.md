# ColorStack chapter sponsorship outreach

## What it does
Built the corporate sponsorship pipeline for a ColorStack campus chapter: company scouting, contact grounding, a benchmarked ask, and sixteen staged outreach drafts.

## How it runs
Sixteen companies were scouted and fit-scored in a sheet, with contact grounding per company. The ask was tiered ($2,500 Community / $5,000 Talent / $10,000 Flagship) after benchmarking against what peer chapters actually charge: NSBE and SHPE chapter sponsorship packets and the school's published corporate tiers. Large employers anchor at $10,000 with a stated $5,000 fallback; smaller firms anchor at $5,000 with $2,500.

Events are framed as benefits the sponsor receives, not obligations. The strongest proof point (four chapter members already interned at or went full-time at one target employer) leads that draft.

## What you get back
A sponsorship sheet with per-company anchors, a template tab with the full tier menu, and sixteen verified drafts staged in Gmail, none sent until approved.
