# Conference trip prep

## What it does
Turns a conference into a recruiting trip: flights researched, recruiters ranked, and outreach prepped before wheels up.

## How it runs
Flight options are priced and ranked, then handed back for the pick. Nothing gets booked; the purchase decision stays with you. Ranked recruiters each get a pre-written LinkedIn DM and an email follow-up in a single doc, ready to send on the road.

## What you get back
A shortlist of flights to choose between and a pre-conference outreach doc, so the trip is spent talking, not typing.
