# Referral verification and tracking

## What it does
Keeps referral asks from being vibes. Each referral is tracked as asked, confirmed, or actually attached to an application.

## How it runs
When a referrer says they submitted, the referral gets verified as attached where verification is possible rather than taken on faith. A confirmed referral is logged against the specific application it supports, so follow-ups reference the real state.

## What you get back
A clear line per referral: who was asked, what they confirmed, and which application it is attached to.
