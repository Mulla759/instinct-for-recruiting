# Standing company watches

## What it does
A dedicated, always-on watch for specific target employers, separate from the general daily sweep. Google careers is the running example.

## How it runs
The watch fires on genuinely new postings in the watched role families, verified open at the official link, then logged to the tracker with a recruiter attached where one exists. Broad triggers catch the whole role family rather than one exact title, so a reworded posting does not slip past.

## What you get back
A ping only when a target company posts something real and relevant. Nothing otherwise.
